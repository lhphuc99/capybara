The UI works from the keyboard: `1` to `4` switch tabs, `/` or Cmd/Ctrl+F goes to the filter, the arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home and End move through requests, logs, storage keys, state changes, cache entries, WebSocket messages and the app menu, and in JSON trees Right and Left open and close a branch, or move into it and back up to its parent. Lists, trees and resize handles also tell screen readers what they are.
